# Competitor & Market Research: Pub Round / Drink Order Apps

## Executive Summary

The pub drink ordering app space splits into two distinct categories: **B2B venue ordering platforms** (Wetherspoons, ROUND, Your Round, Noble) that let customers order from a specific pub's menu and pay digitally, and **personal/social drink tools** (My Drink Order, Drinks Counter/Tally, Boozr, DrinkControl) for individual use. **No app directly solves the "take a round order from friends and remember it at the bar" problem** that MyRound targets. This is a genuine gap in the market.

---

## Category 1: Venue-Specific Ordering Apps (B2B)

These apps are tied to specific pubs/chains. Customers order from the venue's menu, pay in-app, and get table delivery. They solve "skip the queue" but NOT "remember everyone's order."

### 1. Wetherspoons Order & Pay
- **Platform**: iOS, Android
- **What it does**: Order food and drinks to your table at any Wetherspoon pub
- **Key features**:
  - Auto-detects your location and nearest Wetherspoon
  - Browse full food and drink menu
  - Pay via card, Apple Pay, PayPal
  - **Reorder function** for buying rounds (repeat last order)
  - Estimated delivery time shown before ordering
  - Allergen and nutrition info
  - Save favourite Wetherspoon locations
  - Hotel room booking
- **Strengths**: Massive user base, well-tested, fast ordering (under 5 mins), table delivery in 10-15 mins
- **Weaknesses**: Only works at Wetherspoons; drink menus not always up-to-date (missing craft/cask ales); no personal order memory across sessions
- **Rating**: Widely used, one of the most successful pub apps in the UK
- **Relevance to MyRound**: The "reorder" feature is interesting - lets you repeat a previous round. But it's venue-locked and payment-focused, not an order-taking notepad.

Sources: [Wetherspoon App](https://www.jdwetherspoon.com/order-and-pay-app/), [Econsultancy Review](https://econsultancy.com/wetherspoons-order-and-pay-app-review/)

---

### 2. ROUND App (UK's #1 Table Service App)
- **Platform**: iOS, Android, Web
- **What it does**: White-label table ordering for hospitality venues
- **Key features**:
  - Table service, collection/takeaway, and delivery ordering
  - Integrates with all major mobile wallets
  - In-app analytics for venue operators
  - Stock control, reports, and menu management
  - No joining fee or monthly fee (transaction fee only)
  - Setup in under 72 hours for venues
  - 7-day customer support
- **Strengths**: Venue-friendly pricing model; increases dwell time and average order value; reduces serve times
- **Weaknesses**: B2B focused - customers need to use it per-venue; no personal round-tracking
- **Rating**: 4+ stars on Trustpilot; described as "simple to use" and "intuitive"
- **Relevance to MyRound**: Demonstrates demand for digital ordering in pubs. Their success shows people want phone-based ordering. But it's venue-side infrastructure, not a personal tool.

Sources: [ROUND App](https://round.app/table-service-app-pubs-round/), [Trustpilot Reviews](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/round.app)

---

### 3. Your Round
- **Platform**: iOS, Android
- **What it does**: Contactless ordering at participating pubs
- **Key features**:
  - Choose pub, browse menu, order and pay
  - Apple Pay and credit card support
  - Table delivery
  - Free to download
- **Strengths**: Simple concept, no queue
- **Weaknesses**: Significant UX issues - account creation problems, crashes during signup/payment, users charged multiple times for single orders, white-text-on-white-background bugs, no customer support contact visible, clunky signup process
- **Rating**: 4.1/5 (16,483 ratings) but heavily polarised reviews
- **Relevance to MyRound**: Shows that even apps with the word "Round" in the name are solving venue ordering, not personal round-taking. Also a cautionary tale about signup friction - MyRound should have zero signup.

Sources: [Your Round - App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/your-round/id1519451280)

---

### 4. Noble
- **Platform**: iOS
- **What it does**: Order and pay for drinks at venues/events without queuing
- **Key features**:
  - Skip the line ordering at theatres, stadiums, concerts, nightclubs
  - Split tabs
  - Pre-ordering (order before intermission/half-time)
  - Pay via Venmo, Apple Pay, or credit card
  - Secure pickup locations
  - For venues: iPad + receipt printer kit
- **Strengths**: 3-4 minute order-to-drink time; 5% increase in bar revenue; customers spend 50% more via app; works at events not just pubs
- **Weaknesses**: US-focused; venue-dependent; not a personal tool
- **Rating**: Generally positive user reviews for speed
- **Relevance to MyRound**: The pre-ordering concept is smart. Their success proves people want faster drink service. But again, venue-locked.

Sources: [Noble](https://getnoble.co/), [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/business/2019/12/19/boston-based-startup-noble-app-that-lets-fans-skip-line-for-concession-stands/hvPyyEstcwmSm7oAIEGX7K/story.html)

### 5. Greene King / Chef & Brewer / Hungry Horse (Order & Pay)
- **Platform**: iOS, Android
- **What it does**: Table ordering across Greene King's pub portfolio
- **Key features**:
  - Book table, browse menu, order and pay at table
  - Promotion running 02/09/2025 to 31/12/2026
- **Relevance to MyRound**: Another chain-locked ordering app. Confirms the pattern: every major UK pub chain has their own app, but none help you take orders from mates.

Sources: [Greene King Order & Pay](https://www.greeneking.co.uk/order-and-pay-app), [Chef & Brewer](https://www.chefandbrewer.com/order-and-pay-app)

### 6. Pub Order (puborder.app)
- **Platform**: Web (PWA)
- **What it does**: Free QR-code ordering system for independent pubs
- **Key features**:
  - Each pub gets unique subdomain (e.g., redlion.puborder.app)
  - QR code scan → select table → choose products → pay
  - Customisable categories in admin
  - Welcome message for customers
  - Works on any device with a modern browser
  - **Entirely free**
- **Strengths**: Zero cost, works everywhere, no app download needed
- **Weaknesses**: Venue must set it up; customers can't use it independently
- **Relevance to MyRound**: Interesting PWA approach. Proves a web app can work for pub ordering without native app installation. MyRound takes a similar PWA approach but for the customer side.

Sources: [Pub Order](https://puborder.app/)

## Category 2: Personal / Social Drink Tools

These are closer to MyRound's problem space - personal tools for tracking drinks among friends.

### 7. My Drink Order
- **Platform**: iOS, Android
- **What it does**: Display your drink order on your phone screen instead of shouting
- **Key features**:
  - Type in your favourite drink
  - **Large, easily readable text display** shown to bartender
  - Custom messaging ("flash a message across the bar")
  - "Another Round" button
  - "Check Please" button
  - "Ready to Order" for restaurants
- **Strengths**: Solves the noise problem; big readable text is smart; simple concept
- **Weaknesses**: Only for YOUR personal order (not a group); very basic functionality; low ratings/downloads; no memory of previous orders; no group support
- **Rating**: 4.0/5 but only 4 ratings
- **Relevance to MyRound**: **Closest competitor to one aspect of MyRound** - the "show your order at the bar" concept. But it's single-person only, has no round management, no regulars, no grouping. The large-text display idea is validated by this app and worth emulating in the summary screen.

Sources: [My Drink Order - App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/my-drink-order/id6443959789), [Bourquin Group](https://www.bourquingroup.com/my-drink-order/)

---

### 8. Drinks Counter / Tally App
- **Platform**: iOS, Android
- **What it does**: Track drinks consumed in clubs, groups, or personally
- **Key features**:
  - **Group management** with members
  - Members can book/count drinks
  - Group administrators add drinks with name, price, stock, and image
  - Food items supported too
  - History of who booked what and when
  - Cash register function for club finances
  - Group chat with texts, voice recordings, images, surveys/polls
  - Group invoices
  - Groups up to 10 members free
- **Strengths**: Proper group tracking; financial tracking; history; admin controls
- **Weaknesses**: Designed for clubs/societies not pub rounds; overly complex for casual use; setup overhead; focused on billing/accounting not speed
- **Rating**: Functional but niche
- **Relevance to MyRound**: The group concept and member management is relevant. But Tally is optimised for ongoing drink accounting (who owes what over time), not speed-of-order-taking in a noisy pub. MyRound is about the 30-second interaction, not the ledger.

Sources: [Tally App](https://drinkscounter.com/en/tally-app.html), [Drinks Counter](https://drinkscounter.com/en/drinks-counter-app.html), [Club App](https://drinkscounter.com/en/club-app.html)

### 9. Boozr
- **Platform**: iOS, Android
- **What it does**: Social network for pub-goers
- **Key features**:
  - Directory of 700,000+ pubs, bars, clubs worldwide
  - See which friends are at which pubs
  - **Unlimited friend groups** ("Friday Crew", "Work Lot" concept)
  - Full messaging system (1-to-1, group, event-based)
  - Event planning with calendar integration
  - Map-based venue search with filters
  - Privacy-focused: location data not saved, check-ins auto-delete after 4 hours
- **Strengths**: Strong social features; group management; venue discovery; privacy-conscious
- **Weaknesses**: Social network, not an ordering tool; doesn't help with drink orders at all; requires account creation and friend connections
- **Relevance to MyRound**: The **friend groups** concept maps directly to MyRound's "Regular Groups" feature. Boozr proves people want to organise pub visits by friend groups. MyRound could learn from the group naming convention. But Boozr doesn't help at all with the actual ordering problem.

Sources: [Boozr](https://boozr.app/), [Boozr - Pressat](https://pressat.co.uk/releases/boozr-a-social-network-for-pub-regulars-55194356f882604eadee087faaf3b792/), [Tech Digest](https://www.techdigest.tv/2021/03/social-networking-app-for-pub-goers-boozr-launches-on-android-and-ios.html)

### 10. DrinkControl
- **Platform**: iOS
- **What it does**: Personal alcohol consumption tracker
- **Key features**:
  - Track drinks and alcohol expenses
  - Calendar view (sober/in-limits/off-limits/binge days)
  - Dry day streaks
  - Charts, trend-lines, statistics
  - Standard units conversion (glasses, bottles, cans)
  - Calorie tracking
  - Money spent tracking
  - Apple Health sync
  - Personal infographic generation
- **Strengths**: Comprehensive health tracking; beautiful data visualisation; Apple Health integration
- **Weaknesses**: Health/moderation focused, not ordering; single-user only; no group features
- **Relevance to MyRound**: Not a direct competitor. But the drink categorisation and quick-tap interface patterns could inform MyRound's UX. Shows that drink apps succeed when they're fast to log.

Sources: [DrinkControl](https://drinkcontrolapp.com/index.htm), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/drinkcontrol-alcohol-tracker/id456207840)

- **Key features**:

  - Loser (furthest from target time) buys the round
- **Relevance to MyRound**: Only tangentially related - solves "fairness" not "memory". But the concept of round-buying fairness tracking could be a future MyRound feature (track who's bought rounds over time).

## Best Features to Steal / Learn From

### From Wetherspoons
1. **Reorder / "Another Round" button** - repeat the last round with one tap
2. **Estimated time display** - could show "X drinks in this round" as a count

### From My Drink Order
3. **Large, high-contrast text display** - proven concept for showing orders to bartenders in noisy/dark environments
4. **"Another Round" as a first-class action** - single button to repeat

### From Drinks Counter / Tally
5. **Group management with members** - robust group/member model
6. **History of past rounds** - who ordered what and when
7. **Drink images/emojis** - visual identification of drinks

### From Boozr
8. **Unlimited named friend groups** - "Friday Crew", "Work Lot" organisation
9. **Privacy-first approach** - no data leaves the device (MyRound uses localStorage)

### From Noble
10. **Pre-ordering concept** - could pre-build a round before even entering the pub
11. **Speed metrics** - 3-4 minute order time is the benchmark to beat

### From Your Round (negative lessons)
12. **Zero signup friction** - their signup flow is a disaster; MyRound should have NO signup at all
13. **Never charge users multiple times** - offline-first with localStorage avoids payment issues entirely (no payments in MyRound)

### From DrinkControl
14. **Quick-tap drink logging** - fast input is key to adoption
15. **Drink categorisation** - logical grouping makes selection faster

## Unique MyRound Differentiators

1. **The "shout across the room" flow** - no other app optimises for speed-of-input in a noisy environment
2. **Dual summary view** (by-drink for ordering, by-person for handout) - completely unique
3. **Regulars with usual drinks** - one-tap ordering for known friends
4. **Group pre-fill** - add an entire crew's usual drinks with one tap
5. **Works everywhere** - not tied to any venue, chain, or payment system
6. **Zero friction** - PWA, no account, no payment, works offline, installs from browser
7. **Purpose-built for the order-taker** - every other app is for the drinker or the venue; MyRound is for the person going to the bar

---

## Conclusion

MyRound occupies a genuine gap in the market. Every existing app either:
- Requires venue integration (Wetherspoons, ROUND, Your Round, Noble)
- Only handles single-person orders (My Drink Order)
- Is too complex/slow for pub use (Drinks Counter/Tally)
- Solves a social problem not an ordering problem (Boozr)
- Tracks health not orders (DrinkControl)

The combination of **speed-optimised group order taking + regulars memory + dual summary views + offline PWA** is genuinely novel. The closest partial competitor (My Drink Order) validates the large-text-at-bar concept but only handles one person's order at a time.
